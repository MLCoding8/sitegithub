### Hinge 
(SolidWorks)

## Design Process

I create the hinges by extruding the sketch along the axis in which the pin is inserted. Extrusions are made for the holes in the hinges as well as the such that the socket fits together. Then the pin is designed and I put all pats into an assembly and mate them properly.
